# Starlight Learner & Teacher OS - Automation Contract

## Daily capture

The daily automation extracts only material events from the day's available conversations and connected work. It creates a short candidate list for the private Evidence Ledger across learned, built, taught, refined, and guided. It must attach a source or label the item unverified, preserve privacy and consent, avoid whole-life scoring, and never commit personal material to public GitHub.

Output:

1. up to three evidence candidates;
2. one correction or counterevidence item;
3. one promise requiring action or renegotiation;
4. one highest-leverage next practice;
5. a concise human confirmation request before promoting contested or sensitive claims.

## Weekly synthesis

The weekly automation reads the private Notion ledgers, the Drive workbook/field guide, and relevant GitHub changes. It produces a capability and stewardship review, updates private projections, and recommends share-safe public curriculum changes as a proposed GitHub branch or issue only. It must never publish a private profile or identify a guided person without explicit consent.

Output:

1. capability promotions, holds, decay, contests, or retractions;
2. shipped artifacts and verified outcomes;
3. learner/agent transfer and teaching evidence;
4. promise integrity and coordination risks;
5. one active capability bottleneck;
6. one contribution worth making public;
7. one continue/change/park/stop decision;
8. the next week's small set of practices and artifacts.

## Promotion rule

Automations may capture and summarize. They may not confer a credential, publicize private material, or promote a contested claim without the required human assessment and consent.
