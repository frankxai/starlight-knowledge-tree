# Product PRD — Method Explorer & Ecosystem Intelligence Pilot
**Status:** research prototype, not market-validated product. **Scope:** source-backed catalog, retrieval and card contract; no deployment.

## First-principles buyer job
An AI founder/architect must choose tools, frameworks and methods with confidence, without switching among dozens of vendor pages or mistaking marketing for evidence. A creator needs a rights-cleared, reusable explanation that can become a lesson, guide or workflow.

## Market sophistication / positioning
AI directories and search engines already aggregate vendor names. A plain list of companies or acronyms has weak differentiation. Our wedge is **versioned evidence + method-to-capability fit + reproducible implementation + source-aware comparison**. Compete on decision quality, not catalog size. No claims of product-market fit or partner agreements without user and revenue evidence.

## Product / data boundaries
- Public Atlas Knowledge Tree owns authored records and reusable editorial cards. Never import private account data.
- Research Intelligence OS owns methodology and reproducibility protocols.
- Registry owns portfolio identity, decisions, partner status and canonical ownership.
- Starlight Memory owns permission-aware retrieval projections; do not invent a second search authority.
- Vercel is the default experience plane under accepted architecture; Cloudflare stateful coordination only when existing contracts require it.
- Preserve existing web surfaces and source files; no new project, Worker, vector index, D1 or R2 bucket.

## MVP user journey
Search 'ReAct vs RAG' → exact source-linked method cards → compare intended jobs, evidence and limits → open original source → copy a tested ACT-style mission or learning exercise → opt into Academy or research update. No affiliate CTA until agreements and disclosure exist.

## Implementation in this PR
Deterministic zero-dependency catalog `resolve/search/compare/trace/safeCard` over the 12 editorial seed records. It is a **baseline**, not semantic search, production API, partner directory, licensed dataset or finished UX. Every card exposes status and citation links. No unverified record is represented as verified.

## Evidence and acceptance
- 12 draft seed records and 4 namespaced STELLAR meanings remain disambiguated.
- Hermetic tests for exact identity, deterministic retrieval, citations, no fabricated verification, invalid input and unknown IDs.
- Benchmark 20 realistic questions before vector search; require human source inspection and rights review for publication.
- Interviews: 5 AI builders and 5 creator/research users; track task completion, evidence trust, willingness to pay, time saved. These are proposed experiments, not observed results.
- Launch hypothesis: free encyclopedia and comparison pages → €29–€99 curated field guides / skill packs → paid research/evaluation packs for startups where demand exists. Pricing is a hypothesis, not approved SKU.
- Kill/iterate gate: no clear improvement in task success or no willingness to pay after interviews and two small tests; avoid scaling content production until proof.

## IP and scientific publishing
Own original commentary, schemas, benchmark fixtures, visuals and evaluation methods; attribute third-party standards and papers. Track source edition, citation spans, rights, correction/retraction status, replication scope, manuscript version and license. Books and PDFs are reviewed projections from pinned source revisions, not bulk scraped reproductions.
