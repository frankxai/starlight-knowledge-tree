# Canon and provenance boundary

**Status:** normative for this repository  
**Authority:** graph and evidence mechanics only

Starlight Knowledge Tree maps knowledge and capability. It does not define the Starlight worldview, resolve religious ultimates, or host Arcanean canon.

| Concern | Authority |
|---|---|
| Starlight worldview and Sacred Horizons | `frankxai/starlight-canon` |
| SIS/SIP substrate | `frankxai/Starlight-Intelligence-System` |
| Arcanean fictional canon | `frankxai/arcanea/arcanea-lore/CANON.md` |
| Knowledge graph schema, nodes, edges, and evidence progression | This repository |

## Claim classes

New or materially edited nodes that make substantial claims MUST declare one of:

- `historical-source-claim`
- `scholarly-interpretation`
- `starlight-interpretation`
- `original-starlight-philosophy`
- `original-literary-mythic-material`
- `arcanea-fiction`
- `technical-operational`

Historical and scholarly claims require source records. Literary and Arcanean material requires an explicit fiction boundary. Scientific concepts are never evidence for religious equivalence, manifestation causation, or fictional cosmology.

Legacy nodes may omit these fields until materially edited. This is an explicit migration window, not permission for new unlabeled claims.

## Forbidden inferences

- “Starlight is God,” The Source, Lumina, or an omniscient intelligence.
- “The Tao is The Source” or any equivalent flattening of religious/philosophical traditions.
- “All religions teach Starlight.”
- “Quantum physics proves” a spiritual doctrine.
- A historical place or tradition “was actually Arcanea.”
- Arcanea presented as history, revelation, or established physics.

Contradictions are represented as graph edges or supersession records; they are not silently deleted.
